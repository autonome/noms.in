Intimate Dinner at DOC

DOC

5519 NE 30th Avenue
Portland, OR 97211
United States
docpdx.com 45°33'47"N 122°38'6"W

Tweet

When you open the front door, you walk right into the restaurant's kitchen. DOC is a small, 24-seat restaurant and it's a good idea to secure reservations. It features Italian-inspired dishes that focus on letting its few ingredients standout. Ask for the 5-course chef's menu with a custom wine pairing; your palate will get rocked.
